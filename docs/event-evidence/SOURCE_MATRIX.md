# Source responsibilities and clock qualification

All references are current public-documentation inspections preserved in the
[source packet](sources/README.md). These are documented capabilities and design
inferences, **not** successful historical feed qualification. The source of truth
for an event is its publisher and original document version, not a wrapper's
method name. All rows require actual retrieval, ingestion and verification clocks
from our retained logs; historical values remain unknown where no logs exist.

## Clock and identity matrix

| Family / responsible source | Economic/effective clock and native ID | Official publication / scheduled time | First machine observation / our receipt | Update/revision/correction and raw responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Fed decisions/statements** — Board/FOMC publication, implementation note and attachments [M01–M02] | Meeting/action ID; target rate effectiveness can differ from statement release. Keep statement, implementation note, projections and press conference distinct. | Calendar is schedule only. Preserve document's asserted date/time and zone; inspected statement labels 14:00 EDT. Never apply that convention universally to all historical decisions or unscheduled actions. | Actual public-channel/provider observation and collector receipt separately. A release label proves neither; historical receipt remains unknown. | Retain each HTML/PDF/attachment and release ID/URI/hash. Policy replacement is a later action; edited statement is a new version linked to its predecessor. |
| **Fed minutes** — Board/FOMC archive [M01] | Associated meeting dates, distinct minutes publication ID; descriptions of conditions at a meeting are not evidence available at that meeting. | Regular-meeting minutes ordinarily come later; source documents the three-week schedule. Verify the actual release/time/zone for each version, including exceptions. | Require independent channel observations; do not backdate receipt to the meeting or scheduled release. | Preserve original minutes, corrections and attachments. Minutes and statement share meeting linkage, not a single information timestamp. |
| **Fed speeches/testimony** — Board or relevant Reserve Bank, identified separately [M03] | Speech/delivery occasion and speaker; prepared text, delivered words and Q&A are separate evidence. | Preserve web publication label, embargo/release assertion and delivery time where supplied. A date-only speech page remains date-only. A transcript posted later is a later publication unless an earlier retained channel is proven. | Prospectively capture full text or separately licensed contemporaneous feed. No inference of web receipt from start of speech. | Retain versions, media/transcript rights and transcription lineage. Preserve corrections and omissions, not just latest text. |
| **CPI/PPI/employment and other scheduled US macro** — BLS release plus program definitions [B01–B05]; BEA GDP/PCE/income [E01–E04]; FRED/ALFRED as qualified distributor/vintage cross-check [F01–F03] | Series/table, reference month/quarter, SA/NSA, units, base year, estimate stage. These describe the economic period, never the information clock. | Retain archived release with its own date/time/zone, attachments and contemporaneous schedule version. The API observation date/year/period is not publication. GDP advance/second/third estimates are distinct releases. | Original publication clock needs per-release corroboration for historical proxy; observed mode requires collector logs. ALFRED dates and current BLS/BEA API responses do not prove historical reception or release-second ordering. | Retain release tables as published and later revisions. No vintage-date-only event detection: unchanged FRED releases may be absent from that list [F02]. Beyond BLS/BEA (for example Census or Labor weekly claims), qualify the actual publishing agency separately; no blanket US-macro certification. |
| **Revisions/vintages/corrections across macro** — original agency release/erratum; ALFRED bounds as distributor metadata [B02,B04,E02,F01–F03] | Same observation period with new vintage and definition; retain original and revised values. Seasonal/benchmark revisions differ from error corrections. | New publication is when the revised fact becomes information, not its observation period. Date precision remains date precision. FRED real-time bounds are inclusive dates, not our interval convention. | Each revised version gets new observation/receipt evidence. A backfilled vintage has current retrieval and unknown historical receipt. | Match series, units, adjustment and version. Never subtract latest restated prior from original new value; preserve contemporaneously printed prior and previously known prior separately. |
| **Federal rules/regulations/notices** — Federal Register discovery/public inspection; official GovInfo rendition and issuing agency [R01–R03] | Agency, FR document number, docket/RIN, CFR references; proposal, adoption, legal effective date, compliance deadline, enforcement and comment close are distinct claims/events. | FR issue publication date is often date-level. Public-inspection stamp is official filing time; source explicitly warns online posting may be later [R02]. Earlier agency announcement has its own envelope. | Capture web observation/receipt; no equality assumed between filing-room availability and online posting. Preserve later publication independently. | Retain inspection PDF and final official rendition, amendments, withdrawals and correction notices with explicit links. Current eCFR text is a consolidation, not the historical announcement. Verify legal facts against an official edition [R01,R03]. |
| **Tariffs/trade/export controls** — USTR/Presidential/Commerce-BIS actions, official FR legal text, CBP implementation notices where relevant [T01,R01–R03] | Action/docket, tariff/HTS or export-control classification, country, rate/exclusion and entry/effective window. Legal rate change versus import-entry applicability differ. | Announcement, signature, FR publication and customs implementation are separate clocks. USTR's inspected page supplies discovery links; it does not certify historical exact web publication times. | Require channel-specific capture or qualified version archive; no historical receipt inferred from signing/effective date. BIS/CBP candidate pages were inaccessible at the attempted URLs [T02–T04]. | Keep proclamation/order, annexes, exclusion extensions and subsequent amendments. Do not use a current tariff table to reconstruct earlier information without original version evidence. No geopolitical prediction or generated strategy. |
| **Sanctions** — Treasury/OFAC original notices, list changes and relevant legal instruments [O01–O02,R03] | Native list UID/entity IDs, designation/removal/general-license scope and legal effective window; list deletion is not automatically every form of sanctions relief. | OFAC FAQ documents historical changes and recent-actions discovery [O01]. Preserve actual notice precision; a list's current last-updated field is not proof of every past event instant. | Channel evidence required; list search/current snapshot is insufficient. O02 returned an app shell rather than verified delta payloads. | Preserve additions, deletions, modifications and general-license versions. Qualify archive continuity, delta ordering and full-snapshot reconciliation before claiming a reconstructed historical list. |
| **Government awards/contracts** — public awarding-agency announcement; USAspending/FPDS-derived records for corroboration [A01–A04] | Award/PIID/agency/transaction/modification/recipient UEI, signed/action/performance dates and obligated versus potential amount. Contract value is not necessarily revenue or issuer materiality. | Public announcement time/version needs its own evidence; transaction/action and database modification dates are not public release. Official USAspending guidance documents reporting lag and 90-day DOD/USACE procurement delay [A04]. | Only qualify awards with demonstrable public timestamp and raw announcement, or observe them prospectively. Defense announcement candidate returned 403 [A03]; no announcement archive qualified here. | Retain agency notice plus later corrections, amendments, deobligations and award records. Match subsidiaries/joint ventures historically. Do not choose only ex-post market-moving awards. |
| **Issuer earnings/releases/guidance** — issuer IR/original distributor; SEC accession/exhibit as a separate channel [S01–S03] | Issuer and class, fiscal period, accession and exhibit, release/wire story ID. Quarter end and 8-K filing date are not original release time. | Issuer/wire may precede SEC. Store each clock and version separately; SEC acceptance/dissemination cannot establish earlier wire public time. Generic EDGAR APIs update as dissemination occurs [S01]; this is no latency guarantee. | Retain actual channel observations; current EDGAR companyfacts/latest IR page does not prove original release receipt. | Retain original exhibit and earlier issuer/wire bytes where authorized; guidance replacements/corrections append. Form 8-K PDF attempts failed [S02–S03], so no new form-specific legal assertion depends on them. Shared envelope only; no change to existing 10-Q registration. |
| **Financial news** — licensed original publisher/distributor archive or authorized prospective capture [N02–N06] | Publisher/story/version IDs and event cluster; editorial creation, publication and transmission are different meanings requiring provider definitions. A ticker tag is a provider assertion, not a historical class master. | Require documented created/published/updated/transmit semantics, native offset and precision. Benzinga is an illustrative documented candidate, not a selected or licensed source. | Record actual complete-message receipt, sequence/gaps/reconnects; provider timestamps are not ours. Bounded cached stream replay cannot establish historical full-version coverage. | Retain all authorized original/update/retraction payloads and duplicate provenance. Benzinga documents lifecycle actions [N06] and removal/update synchronization [N03], but full history of every version, entitlements and replay/AI rights remain unqualified. Current search/date filters cannot recreate a wire. |

## Official APIs versus clients

| Candidate | Observed responsibility | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FRED/ALFRED official API [F01–F03] | Explicit real-time/vintage query bounds and series metadata. Defaults can select today's revised view. | Pin vintage/query/output type and reconcile observations/release inventory. A first-release query is not an intraday receipt archive. Do not assume every series has full original history. |
| Community `fredapi`, pinned `d0a0ba3001ebbbceafdcbdd3eb23828f0537cdff` [W01–W02] | Inspected `get_series_all_releases` and `get_series_as_of_date`; latter filters all rows to revisions on/before date. | Independent wrapper, not official Fed SDK. Its as-of helper returns potentially multiple revisions per observation despite its convenience description. Select last admissible vintage explicitly, and quarantine unorderable same-day revisions. No client installed or API key used. |
| BEA official API and agency-linked `beaapi`, pinned `0d44ba252c5fbb8147656631f907dcfe598454d4` [E01,E04,W03–W04] | Agency-linked Python/R client and official data retrieval parameters. Metadata observes recent commits, not runtime support guarantees. | Transport and dataframe convenience do not certify historical versions/receipts. Match archived release/vintage files and exact dataset semantics; do not assume a `Year` parameter selects an information vintage. No client installed or data API called. |
| BLS official API [B01] | Series/year/period/value and footnotes; latest/current published time-series access. | The inspected response contract does not demonstrate original-version/receipt replay. Use release archives plus per-program vintage qualification; no need to add a community wrapper as scientific authority. |
| Federal Register / GovInfo [R01–R03] | Discovery, inspection and legal-rendition responsibilities split explicitly. | An unofficial XML representation or reachable REST endpoint is not the legal original or a machine-availability guarantee. Interactive OpenAPI discovery was not successfully retrieved [R04]. |

The pins above identify inspected source, not dependencies or endorsement. The
capture manifest records current commit metadata and failures. Maintained clients
may reduce later transport work; none supplies missing scientific evidence.
